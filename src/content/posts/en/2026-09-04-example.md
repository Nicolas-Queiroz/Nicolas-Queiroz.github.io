## How the bot works

Every two days, a GitHub Action runs a Python script that:

1. Fetches relevant news from Currents API using keywords from your professional profile.
2. Picks the most recent and relevant one.
3. Sends it to Gemini to generate an original post in both Portuguese and English.
4. Saves files to `drafts/` for you to review before publishing.

## Next steps
